## Trial, purchase and activation

The trial is requested by the account holder through hosted Stripe Checkout and is limited to one permitted trial per account under server enforcement. It is not a cardless or 14-day offer. State the exact trial end/first monthly charge before consent; give legally required reminders. An authorization hold/payment-provider check may apply only as disclosed by the provider. A checkout return, local flag or receipt screenshot does not activate access: the server verifies current subscription state and configured prices. Unavailable billing configuration refuses Checkout rather than substituting a different price.

## Renewal and cancellation

A recurring subscription renews for its disclosed interval unless canceled through the authenticated hosted billing portal before the next renewal. Uninstalling CROT or disconnecting a social account does not cancel billing. A cancel-at-period-end subscription remains active until its verified paid period ends; actual canceled/expired/past-due state can close Relay and paid publication admission. A downgrade does not remove registered local projects or disable local agents/chat, L1 or audit. Adding beyond the Free cap requires a fresh eligible entitlement. Offline/unknown cloud status does not grant paid publication or remote access, but does not convert security into a paid feature. Charges and termination remedies remain subject to mandatory law.
